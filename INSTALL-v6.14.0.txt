AGCORE v6.14.0

This release does not require a database migration.

Deploy from the folder containing package.json:

npm install
npx vercel --prod

After deployment:
1. Select the intended season in the top bar.
2. Open Transport Log and download a fresh Excel logsheet. Existing files cannot gain the repaired dropdown lists.
3. Confirm the Farm · Field, date, destination, vehicle, grade, status and completion dropdowns are present.
4. Check Weather: all spray outputs are explicitly presented as decision-support recommendations.
5. Open Terms and confirm the legal version is 2026-10-14.

Important:
- Keep the Lists worksheet in the transport workbook; it is intentionally hidden and supplies the dropdowns.
- AGCORE's legal text is a product-risk safeguard, not a substitute for review by a South African attorney.
