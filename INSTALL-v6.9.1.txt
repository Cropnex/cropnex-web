Correct reporting workflow
1. Every planned field activity contributes its planned_total to projected costs.
2. When an activity is actualised, that activity switches from planned_total to actual_total.
3. Other field expenses recorded in Financials are added to the activity costs.
4. Reconciled Transport Log tonnes are priced FIFO against physical grain contracts.
5. Tonnes remaining after contracts use the latest farmer SAFEX planning reference.
6. Field income, costs, cost/ha and margin all come from one field_performance_report view.

Additional fixes
- Opening Reports automatically refreshes contract/SAFEX allocations before loading figures.
- Saving a SAFEX reference immediately reprices existing reconciled tonnes.
- Saving a grain contract immediately reprices existing reconciled tonnes.
- Live Harvest Performance shows blended price, projected income, activity costs and margin.
- Field Performance explains contracted tonnes, SAFEX tonnes, actualised costs and costs still planned.

After deployment
- Open Grain Marketing and confirm a SAFEX planning price exists for the crop.
- Confirm contracts use the same season and a compatible commodity name.
- Open Reports and press Refresh Reports.
