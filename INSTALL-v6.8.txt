AGCORE v6.8 INSTALLATION

1. Replace the project files with this package.
2. In Supabase SQL Editor, run:
   supabase/migrations/202610020001_farm_records_scanner_and_pricing.sql
3. In Vercel > Project > Settings > Environment Variables, add:
   OPENAI_API_KEY = your private OpenAI API key
   OPENAI_SCANNER_MODEL = gpt-4o-mini   (optional)
4. Redeploy the Production deployment.

New in v6.8
- Diesel Logbook with Equipment, Activity and Field dropdowns, L/hour tracking and Excel export.
- Manual rainfall gauges, daily readings and seasonal history.
- AI Scanner review workflow for Transport delivery notes and Inventory receipts.
- Farmer planning price per crop and season, combined with signed grain contracts for revenue forecasts.

Important
- Never add OPENAI_API_KEY as NEXT_PUBLIC_OPENAI_API_KEY. It must remain a server-only secret.
- The scanner always requires a farmer to review and confirm extracted information.
- The diesel export supports record keeping; confirm tax requirements with a qualified practitioner.
