AGCORE v6.9.0 - Multi-field actuals, Harvest summaries and catalogue tools

IMPORTANT: Run this SQL migration in Supabase before testing:
supabase/migrations/202610040001_harvest_plans_and_admin_hectares.sql

Included
- Fixed planned activities showing the first inventory product more than once during actualisation.
- Product names are shown above each actual product selector.
- Actualise the same planned activity across several fields from Farm Planning.
- Harvest History now groups loads by field and calculates accumulated tonnes, yield, weighted moisture, average price and revenue.
- Every field and season receives a standard Harvest planned activity.
- Reconciled Transport Log data continues to feed Harvest records and field yield.
- Administrators can export the complete Master Data workbook, edit classifications and import it again.
- Administration shows hectares and field counts for every registered account plus the total hectares on AGCORE.
- Scouting photos can be assessed by AI for possible symptoms, causes, severity and treatment classes.

Scouting safety
- AI suggestions are advisory and must be confirmed by a qualified agronomist and registered product label.
- The first version suggests active ingredients or treatment classes, not sponsored commercial brands.
- Sponsored partner products should be visibly labelled and checked against crop registrations before launch.

Deployment
1. Replace the project files.
2. Run the SQL migration above once in Supabase SQL Editor.
3. Commit and push to Vercel.
