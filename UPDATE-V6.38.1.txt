CropNex v6.38.1 — Administrator-only Master Data

Adviser registration
1. Register using the normal CropNex sign-up page and verify the email.
2. In Account verification, request Agronomist or Chemical Representative.
3. A CropNex administrator approves that role in the Verification Centre.
4. Each farmer invites the approved adviser by email to specific fields under
   Agronomy & Advisory > Connections. The adviser accepts the invitation there.
5. The adviser can work on those shared fields across clients. Approval of the
   account alone does not grant access to farmers' fields or finance.

Master Data
- The Master Data page is now only under Administration.
- Only CropNex administrators may open it. Farmer, adviser, employee and
  organisation administrator accounts cannot open it, even with a saved old
  dashboard grant or a direct URL. Non-admins never mount its data-loading workspace.
- Master Data is no longer offered as an assignable organisation dashboard.
- Existing database permissions already restrict shared master-product editing
  and product-request approval to CropNex administrators; they remain in force.
- Approved products remain selectable in planning, inventory and other permitted
  workflows. Farm-specific operations/rates in Farm Setup are separate records;
  their existing ownership rules are unchanged.
- Master workbook import/export and request review remain available to admins.
