AGCORE v6.10.0 - Yield expectations and whole-business forecasting

1. In Supabase SQL Editor, run this migration after the v6.9.1 migration:
   supabase/migrations/202610040003_yield_expectations_and_commitments.sql

2. Deploy this complete project to Vercel.

WHAT CHANGED
- SAFEX now includes a Current Yield Expectation per crop and season.
- Expected hectares x yield is valued using signed contracts first and the farmer's planning SAFEX price for the remaining tonnes.
- Field reports use planned activity costs until actualised costs become available.
- Business & Finance now reads the same reporting engine as Reports.
- Equipment records include installment amount, frequency and finance term in seasons.
- General business commitments support living expenses, insurance, rent, salaries, loans and other recurring costs.
- Reports show both field gross margin and the projected business margin after equipment and general commitments.

IMPORTANT
- Keep yield crop names aligned with the crop used in field planning. Maize variants are matched automatically.
- Equipment installments are added in Resources > Equipment.
- Non-field commitments are added in Business > Business & Finance.
- The forecast is a planning estimate, not an accounting statement. Booked transactions remain visible separately.
