CropNex v6.38.0 — Agronomy & Advisory pilot
Prepared 2 November 2026

This is the FULL project, including all existing app pages, public assets,
components, libraries, database migrations and tests. Dependency folders,
generated build output, Git history and private environment files are excluded.
Keep your existing .git folder and local environment files when replacing the
project files in Codespaces. Vercel environment variables remain in Vercel.

GET STARTED
1. The adviser creates a CropNex account and requests Agronomist or Chemical
   Representative access. The CropNex administrator approves that role in the
   existing Verification Centre. Account approval remains manual.
2. The farmer opens Farm Map, clicks a field and selects Request agronomy service.
   Alternatively open Agronomy & Advisory under Insights and choose a field.
3. Under Connections, invite the approved adviser by email. Optionally share
   this field's activity plans, scouting observations or harvest history.
   This does not grant finance access or access to other fields.
4. The adviser signs in, opens Agronomy & Advisory and accepts the invitation.
   Advisers see their permitted fields across clients, independently of their
   own farm-season selection. They do not need to be made Farmers or admins.
5. Create a request for chemical soil grids, soil classification, leaf analysis,
   soil sampling, crop scouting, field inspection or another service. A saved
   boundary is required. Field boundary, hectares, crop, cultivar and season
   are captured with the request. Assign an active adviser immediately or later.
6. The farmer or assigned adviser updates request status and scheduled date.
   Record visits, descriptions, photographs and results against the field and,
   optionally, the related request. Completed/cancelled requests remain history.
7. The adviser creates a recommendation with products, seed or operations,
   rates per hectare, unit prices, work date and instructions. Save a private
   draft or submit it to the farmer. Submitted versions cannot be silently edited.
8. A farmer with advisory editing AND planning approval access can approve,
   return or decline. Approval creates the costed activity plan once using the
   CURRENT field hectares and season. It does not actualise work or deduct stock.
   Open Activities & Planning to review the plan and follow normal actualisation.

REQUESTS AND RESULTS
- Invitations and requests appear inside CropNex. This release does not send
  automatic emails, SMS or WhatsApp notifications and does not book a paid lab.
- Map location and GeoJSON boundary download are available for the field.
- Upload private PDF, CSV, JPG, PNG, WebP, GeoJSON/JSON, ZIP or TIFF files up to 20 MB.
  Choose Prepare private attachment, then Open attachment. Prepared download
  links last five minutes; prepare a new link after expiry.
- Grid CSV template is available in the upload window. Required columns:
  sample_id,latitude,longitude,parameter,value,unit,depth_cm,sample_date
  Use decimal coordinates and YYYY-MM-DD dates. Each parameter/unit/depth/date
  combination becomes a separate measured-sample layer. Different sampling
  rounds and units are not combined. Outside-boundary samples require confirmation.
- Select Show grid results on field to see the measured sampling locations.
  Colours show relative values, not sufficiency classes or interpolated zones.
  PDFs, GeoTIFFs, classifications and zipped GIS files are retained as supporting
  attachments; this pilot does not render those formats as map overlays.
- Connections are per field. Revoking a connection blocks subsequent reads and
  writes, and prevents approval of that adviser's submitted recommendations.
  Previously downloaded files cannot be recalled; already prepared links may
  remain usable until their five-minute expiry.
- Organisation members can have view or edit access to the Advisory dashboard.
  Existing field dashboard grants are copied to Advisory by the migration,
  preserving view/edit distinctions. Employees remain view-only unless their
  organisation role and explicit permissions are changed through administration.

ONE NEW MIGRATION
After the earlier v6.37.0 migrations have succeeded, run only:
supabase/migrations/202610300001_agronomy_advisory.sql

An identical standalone copy is provided as CropNex-v6.38.0-Migration.sql.
Use one copy, not both. In Supabase SQL Editor paste the ENTIRE new migration
and run it as a single query. It enables Row Level Security on every new table,
creates a private attachment bucket, extends approved roles and adds checked
workflow functions. It does not drop existing operational tables or records.
Do not rerun earlier migrations that already succeeded.
This workspace has NOT applied the migration to your live Supabase project.
No new environment variable or service-role key is needed for this feature.

Keep your existing launch setting and account-approval policy unchanged.
